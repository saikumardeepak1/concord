# Subscription Changes

## Upgrades

Plan upgrades take effect immediately. The customer is charged a prorated
amount for the remainder of the current billing period at the new plan's rate.
Features unlock instantly.

## Downgrades

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. The customer
retains all features of the current plan until the period ends. No proration
is issued for downgrades; this prevents abuse of upgrade-then-downgrade.

## Pausing

Annual customers may pause their subscription once per year for up to 60 days.
Monthly customers cannot pause; they must cancel and re-subscribe. During a
pause, the account is read-only: no new data, no API access.

## Cancellation

Customers can self-serve cancel from **Settings -> Billing -> Plan**. The
subscription remains active until period end. After cancellation, the account
enters a 30-day grace period during which data can be exported. After 30 days,
data is permanently deleted per the data retention policy.

## Plan tiers

- **Free**: 1 user, 1k API calls/month, community support, no SLA.
- **Pro**: 10 users included, 100k API calls, business-hours email support.
- **Enterprise**: unlimited users, custom limits, 24/7 support, contractual SLA.

Custom enterprise pricing is negotiated by sales, not support. Support agents
should refer enterprise pricing requests to sales rather than quoting numbers.
