# Refund Policy

## Overview

Acme SaaS offers refunds under specific, documented conditions. All refund
decisions are governed by this policy and exceptions require manager approval.

## Standard Refund Window

Customers may request a full refund within **14 days** of any new subscription
purchase or annual renewal. The refund must be requested by the billing contact
on the account.

- Monthly subscriptions: 14 days from charge date.
- Annual subscriptions: 14 days from renewal date, prorated for unused time.
- Add-ons and one-time charges: 14 days from charge date.

## Out-of-Window Refunds

Refunds outside the 14-day window are not granted automatically. The following
conditions may justify an exception:

1. **Confirmed billing error** (duplicate charge, wrong amount, charged after
   cancellation): refund the disputed amount in full, no manager approval
   required. Maximum auto-approval is $200; above that, escalate.
2. **Service-level credit** (documented outage exceeding SLA): credit, not cash
   refund. Calculated as 10x the prorated downtime.
3. **Goodwill** (long-tenured customer, isolated complaint): max $100,
   requires manager approval.

## Exclusions

Refunds will not be issued for:

- Usage-based charges that were correctly metered.
- Subscriptions on free trials that auto-converted with prior email notice.
- Plans cancelled mid-cycle (service continues to period end; no proration).
- Chargebacks already initiated with the card issuer.

## Refund Limits

- Single auto-approved refund cap: **$200 USD**.
- Daily customer cap (sum of all refunds): **$500 USD**.
- Beyond these limits, escalate to a human billing specialist.

## Processing Time

Refunds appear on the customer's statement within 5-10 business days. Same-day
processing is not available.
