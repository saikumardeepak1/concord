# Credits, Adjustments, and Discounts

## Account credits

Credits applied to an Acme account come from three sources:

1. **Service-level credits** — automatic when uptime falls below the SLA.
2. **Manual adjustments** — issued by support for confirmed billing errors
   under $200, or by managers for goodwill within policy limits.
3. **Promotional credits** — applied during sales campaigns or onboarding.

## Credit expiration

- **Service credits**: never expire while the subscription is active.
- **Goodwill credits**: expire 12 months after issuance if unused.
- **Promotional credits**: expiration date is shown on the credit line; if
  no date is shown, default is 6 months.

Credits are applied automatically to the next invoice. Customers can see all
active credits and their expiration dates at **Settings -> Billing -> Credits**.

## Small adjustments

Small unexplained adjustments (typically under $5) on a statement usually fall
into one of these categories:

- **Proration rounding**: when seats change mid-cycle, the prorated charge or
  credit is rounded to the nearest cent. Cumulative rounding sometimes shows
  as a small line item on the next invoice.
- **Tax recalculation**: if tax rates change in the customer's jurisdiction
  between invoices, a small adjustment line appears.
- **Currency conversion**: customers billed in a non-USD currency may see a
  tiny adjustment reflecting the FX rate change between charge and settlement.

Customers can always view the full adjustment line item in their invoice PDF;
the audit trail explains the source.

## Discounts

- **Annual prepay discount**: customers who pay annually receive **15% off**
  the equivalent monthly price. This is automatic when selecting an annual
  plan at checkout.
- **Non-profit discount**: verified 501(c)(3) organizations receive **30% off**
  on Pro and Enterprise plans. Submit verification through the support portal.
- **Educational discount**: accredited educational institutions receive
  **50% off** with .edu domain verification.
- **Volume discounts** (Enterprise only): negotiated by sales on a per-deal
  basis. Support agents should refer enterprise volume pricing to sales.

Discounts do not stack. The largest applicable discount applies.

## How credits interact with refunds

Credits are applied before any refund is calculated. If a customer's account
has $50 in credits and they're eligible for a $100 refund, the refund is
issued for $100 cash; the credits remain available for future invoices unless
the customer specifically requests the credits be converted (which is not the
default).
